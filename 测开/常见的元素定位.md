## 元素定位

- ### By.LINK_TEXT

>### 源码
>
>```html
><a href="/register" class="btn btn-outline-primary btn-sm mt-2">
>       <i class="fas fa-user-plus"></i> Create Account
></a>
>```
>
>### 定位
>
>```python
>register_button = (By.LINK_TEXT, "Create Account")
>```

- ### BY.ID

  

- ### BY.CLASS_NAME

> ### 源码
>
> ```html
> <button type="submit" class="btn btn-primary btn-block btn-lg mt-3">
>                   <i class="fas fa-sign-in-alt"></i> Sign In
> </button>
> ```
>
> ### 定位
>
> ```python
> login_button = (By.CLASS_NAME, "btn")   # 不要选择全部的 btn，可能会有多个
> ```

- ### BY.XPATH

> ### 源码
>
> ```html
> <a class="btn btn-outline-light btn-md" href="/logout">
>                 <i class="fas fa-sign-out-alt"></i> Logout
> </a>
> ```
>
> ### 定位
>
> ```python
> logout_button = (By.XPATH,  "//a[contains(@href,'logout')]")
> ```
>
> ​

> ## 双定位
>
> ### 源码
>
> ```html
> <div class="alert alert-success alert-dismissible fade show" role="alert">
>                     <strong>Success!</strong> Account created successfully for username100000! Please sign in to continue.
>                     <button type="button" class="close" data-dismiss="alert" aria-label="Close">
>                       <span aria-hidden="true">×</span>
> </button>
> </div>
> ```
>
> ### 定位
>
> ```python
> is_display_Success = (By.XPATH, "//div[contains(@class, 'alert-success') and 											contains(@role, 'alert')]")
> ```
